YOUR STARTER PLAN✿

Classic diya circle

A welcoming design with a simple circular centre and petal details.

◷ 30–45 min✦ Easy–medium

What you'll need

  • 4–5 colours of rangoli powder
  • Chalk or pencil for the outline
  • A small bowl or stencil for circles
  • Optional flower petals and 2 diyas

Make it in 4 steps

  1. Clean and dry the floor; mark a light circle.
  2. Draw a small centre flower or diya shape.
  3. Add 8–12 petals around the circle and fill each section.
  4. Outline with white powder and add flowers or diyas safely.
